TROUBLESHOOTING

Quick fixes for common problems

The APK will not install

Allow installation from unknown sources for Downloader in device settings, then try again.

Login failed

Check upper and lower case, remove spaces and confirm the server URL is exact.

Playback is buffering

Restart your router and device, use Ethernet or 5 GHz Wi‑Fi and test another player.

Connection is blocked

Try a different internet connection. If your ISP blocks it, enable a trusted VPN and reopen the player.

A second device does not work

Your package supports 3 simultaneous connections. In TiviMate choose another Device # for every additional device.

IPTV VPN guide

If you have connection problems or your internet provider blocks access to services, a VPN can often help. Choose your device and follow the simple steps below.

Choose deviceTelegram @dzekcano72

Choose your device

The recommended VPN in this guide is Proton VPN. Installation differs slightly by device.

📱 Android phone / tablet🍎 iPhone / iPad📺 Android TV / TV Box🔥 Fire TV / Firestick💻 Windows computer
📱 Android phone / tablet

Install Proton VPN, sign in and connect to an available server before reopening your IPTV app.

  1. Install Proton VPN from Google Play.
  2. Open the app and sign in or create a Proton account.
  3. Tap Quick Connect or choose a server.
  4. Approve the VPN request the first time you connect.
  5. When connected, reopen your IPTV app.
🍎 iPhone / iPad
  1. Install Proton VPN from the App Store.
  2. Open the app and sign in or create an account.
  3. Choose Quick Connect.
  4. When iOS asks for permission to add a VPN configuration, tap Allow.
  5. After connecting, reopen your IPTV app.
📺 Android TV / TV Box
  1. Open Google Play Store on your TV device.
  2. Search for Proton VPN and install it.
  3. Open the app; a sign-in code may appear on screen.
  4. On a phone or computer open protonvpn.com/tv, sign in and enter the TV code.
  5. On the TV choose a server or Quick Connect.
  6. Once connected, reopen your IPTV app.
🔥 Fire TV / Firestick
  1. Open the Appstore on your Fire TV / Firestick.
  2. Find and install Proton VPN.
  3. Open the app and follow the sign-in instructions.
  4. If a code appears, open protonvpn.com/tv on another device and enter it.
  5. Connect to a VPN server and reopen your IPTV app.
💻 Windows computer
  1. Download Proton VPN from the official Windows page.
  2. Run the installer and finish setup.
  3. Open Proton VPN and sign in.
  4. Click Quick Connect or choose a server manually.
  5. After connecting, reopen your IPTV app.
